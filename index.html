        Tags

        Weight 5 matters most. Tags with weight 4 or 5 search for an exact subject. Tags with weight 1 to 3 search for a similar subject. Tick Exclude to lower the score of books that have that tag.

          Blacklist

          Type a word or a sentence to blacklist. Find books never searches a tag that contains it. It also hides any book whose title, author or subjects contain it. Matching ignores capital letters and punctuation, and looks for whole words.

            Blacklisted books

            Books you blacklist from Find books are listed here. A blacklisted book is never recommended. Remove it from this list to allow it again.

              Find books

              Searches your tags, heaviest first, then pairs of your top five; books must match two tags. Scores use tag weights, ratings (less with few votes), small boosts for newer books, owned authors and sequels, and penalise very popular books. Top 50 become 20, max two per author.

              You can change the style after a search. The shelf is re-ordered without a new search.

                    Import or export library

                    Export saves the books in your library as a file you can keep or move to another browser. Import adds books from such a file. Books already in your library are skipped, and nothing is removed.
