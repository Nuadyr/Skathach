# Skathach

Build a library of books you know, tag it, then find more books to read.

**Live:** https://nuadyr.github.io/Skathach/

## What it does

- **Library:** add books by title, author, series or ISBN. Mark any you did not finish.
- **Tags:** tags come from your books' subjects. Weight them 1 to 5, or exclude them.
- **Blacklist:** hide words or phrases from searches and results.
- **Find books:** searches Open Library using your tags, scores the results, and builds a shelf of 20. Choose Discover (more variety) or Continue (closer to what you read).

## Use it

1. Open the link above.
2. Add a few books.
3. Adjust your tags.
4. Press Find books.

No install, no account, no build step.

## Data and privacy

- Your library, tags and last results stay in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server of ours.
- Searches go to [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org).
- Previews load from Google Books only when you open one.

## Run locally

It is one file. Download `index.html` and open it in a browser.
